About the Project

VirasatAI

A student innovation project that blends AI and cultural preservation.

🎯

Our Mission

To make India's rich cultural heritage accessible and engaging through cutting-edge AI technology. We believe that understanding our heritage is the first step to preserving it for future generations.

🧠

How It Works

VirasatAI uses a TensorFlow.js-powered image recognition model trained on Indian heritage sites. Point your camera → AI identifies the site → Our curated database reveals its complete cultural story, including history, cuisine, art forms, and local artisan traditions.

⚡

Technology

TensorFlow.js MobileNet HTML5 Canvas SVG Maps MediaDevices API Vanilla JavaScript CSS3 Animations
🌏

Impact

By digitally documenting and showcasing heritage sites with AI, VirasatAI contributes to cultural preservation, promotes heritage tourism, and educates the next generation about India's incredible diversity.
